About JegedEdge

Years Of Experience In Hair cuts!

At JegedEdge Hair Care and Barbering Services, we specialize in providing top-tier hair care and barbering services designed specifically for clients and personnel in rehab/nursing homes and group homes and beyond.

Our mission is to enhance the well-being and self-esteem of individuals in care facilities by offering a holistic and premium grooming experience.

We focus on delivering services that promote confidence, relaxation, and personal care in a professional and compassionate environment.
